简要说明：简体仓颉字典方块不会收集、存储或分享任何可识别您身份的个人资料。所有学习进度、游戏记录与应用设置均只存储在您的设备上，开发者无法访问。

1. 我们收集的资料

本应用不会通过互联网收集任何个人资料。我们不会要求您注册账号，也不会跟踪您的使用行为。

为了提供学习与游戏功能，以下资料会仅存储在您的设备本地：

  • 仓颉学习进度（已学会的字、错字本、常用字练习状态）
  • 游戏与练习记录（分数、准确率、连击、游玩时间等）
  • 应用设置（提示显示、仓颉码显示、音效开关）
  • 搜索记录与收藏字词
  • 您自定义的显示名称（例如「用户1」）

2. 资料的使用方式

上述资料只用于以下目的：

  • 在应用内显示您的学习统计与进度
  • 记录游戏与练习成绩，供您查看历史记录
  • 根据您的设定调整应用行为

3. 资料的存储与安全

所有资料均使用 Apple 的标准本地存储机制（通过 React Native AsyncStorage）存储在您的设备上。资料不会上传至任何服务器，开发者亦无法访问。

当您删除应用时，所有本地资料将一并被移除。

4. 第三方服务

本应用使用以下技术框架，但它们不会因此收集您的个人资料：

  • Expo / React Native：用于构建与运行应用。
  • Expo Updates：仅用于下载应用更新文件，不会收集个人资料。

本应用不包含广告、分析跟踪或社交媒体 SDK。

5. 儿童隐私

本应用适合所有年龄层使用。我们不会明知而收集 13 岁以下儿童的个人资料。由于我们不收集任何个人资料，家长可放心让儿童使用。

6. 您的权利

您可以随时通过以下方式删除您的资料：

  • 在应用内清除游戏记录或练习进度。
  • 删除应用以移除所有本地存储的资料。

7. 隐私政策变更

我们可能会因应法律要求或应用功能更新而修订本政策。任何重大变更将于此页面公布，并更新生效日期。

Privacy Policy

Summary: Simplified Cangjie Dictionary Blocks does not collect, store, or share any personally identifiable information. All learning progress, game records, and app settings are stored locally on your device and are inaccessible to the developer.

1. Information We Collect

This app does not collect any personal data over the internet. We do not require account registration and we do not track your usage.

To provide the learning and game features, the following data is stored locally on your device only:

  • Cangjie learning progress (learned characters, wrong-character list, common-character practice status)
  • Game and practice records (scores, accuracy, combos, play time)
  • App settings (hint display, Cangjie code display, sound toggle)
  • Search history and favorite characters
  • Your custom display name (e.g., "User 1")

2. How We Use Information

The above data is used solely to:

  • Display your learning statistics and progress within the app
  • Record game and practice scores for your history
  • Adjust app behavior according to your settings

3. Data Storage and Security

All data is stored on your device using standard Apple local storage (via React Native AsyncStorage). No data is uploaded to any server, and the developer cannot access it.

Deleting the app will remove all locally stored data.

4. Third-Party Services

This app uses the following technology frameworks, which do not collect your personal information:

  • Expo / React Native: Used to build and run the app.
  • Expo Updates: Used solely to download app update bundles; no personal data is collected.

This app does not contain advertising, analytics tracking, or social media SDKs.

5. Children's Privacy

This app is suitable for all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. Since we do not collect any personal data, parents can let children use the app with confidence.

6. Your Rights

You can delete your data at any time by:

  • Clearing game records or practice progress inside the app.
  • Deleting the app to remove all locally stored data.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy to comply with legal requirements or app feature changes. Any significant changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
