Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 20, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the mobile application Time Survival ("the App", "we", "our", or "us") handles information. By using the App, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

Time Survival is designed with privacy in mind. The App does not collect, transmit, or share any personal information. Specifically:

  • We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, or any other contact information.
  • We do not collect precise or approximate location data.
  • We do not collect device identifiers, advertising IDs, or usage analytics.
  • We do not use third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs.
  • We do not sell or share data with third parties.

2. Data Stored Locally on Your Device

Your game progress, settings, and preferences are stored locally on your device only (for example, in on-device storage). This data never leaves your device and is not transmitted to us or any third party. If you delete the App, all locally stored data is permanently removed.

3. In-App Purchases

The App offers an optional in-app purchase. All purchases are processed entirely by Apple through the App Store and StoreKit. We never see or store your payment information. Purchase handling is subject to Apple's Privacy Policy. Purchase restoration and transaction history are managed by Apple and can be accessed through your Apple ID settings.

4. Children's Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, including children under the age of 13. Since the App collects no personal data at all, it complies with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and similar regulations by design.

5. Third-Party Services

The App does not integrate any third-party services that collect personal data. The only external service involved is Apple's App Store, which handles in-app purchase transactions as described in Section 3.

6. Data Security

Because the App does not collect or transmit any personal information to external servers, there is no personal data at risk of breach on our side. All data remains under your control on your device.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date. Continued use of the App after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
